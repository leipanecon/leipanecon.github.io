About

I am currently an Assistant Professor (Lecturer) in Economics and was the Economics Honours Course Lead in the School of Accounting, Economics and Finance at Curtin University, Australia. I am also a Non-resident Research Fellow at the Centre for Development Economics and Sustainability (CDES), Monash University, Australia, and an External Research Fellow at the Centre for Global Finance (CGF), SOAS University of London. I was an Adjunct Faculty at Soochow University, Taiwan. Prior to joining Curtin University, I was an Adjunct Assistant Professor (Lecturer) in Economics in the School of Arts at Australian Catholic University.

I obtained my Ph.D. in Economics from Monash University, Australia. My research focuses on international economics, monetary economics and financial economics, with particular attention to exchange rate dynamics and monetary policy. I am also deeply interested in financial economics. My work investigates the intersection of macroeconomic policy and global financial markets, aiming to offer insights that address both theoretical and practical challenges in our increasingly interconnected world.
